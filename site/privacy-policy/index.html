Privacy Policy

Short version: this site does not track you.

Who I am

This is the personal portfolio of Aryan Singh, based in Delhi, India. You can reach me at as6855821@gmail.com.

What this site collects

Nothing. There are no cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels and no user accounts. I do not run any scripts that profile visitors.

The contact form

The form on the contact page does not send your message to a server. When you press Submit, it opens your own email app with a draft addressed to me. Nothing leaves your device until you send that email yourself. Your email address and message then reach me the same way any email does.

Hosting and logs

The site is served by a hosting provider. Like every web host, it may keep standard technical logs (IP address, browser, time of request) for security and reliability. I do not use these logs to identify visitors.

Links to other sites

Pages link to GitHub, LinkedIn, Instagram, WhatsApp and to live project sites. Those services have their own privacy policies and I do not control them.

Your rights

If you have emailed me and want your messages deleted, write to me and I will delete them.
